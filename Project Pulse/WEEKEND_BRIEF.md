# PULSE weekend research and build brief

**CodeJam 16 · Saturday, November 21, 2026 · GitHub submission and edit cutoff Sunday, November 22 at noon Montréal time**

## Technical choices for the weekend

1. **Pose and movement.** Start with MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite in browser video mode. Bundle the model and matching WASM locally and warm up before the first count-in. Use visible shoulders, elbows and wrists for the first move, a rhythmic arm press. Use shoulder-relative features and each player's comfortable range, with a confidence gate and simple hysteresis for repetitions. Throttle inference and use a worker if it blocks the render loop. MoveNet Lightning is a credible 17-keypoint alternative if MediaPipe fails an early device test. Prefer camera-free mode for the immediate rehearsal fallback. [MediaPipe web guide](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker?tab=Web) · [Google's MoveNet release and browser demo](https://blog.tensorflow.org/2021/05/next-generation-pose-detection-with-movenet-and-tensorflowjs.html)
2. **Timing.** Use Web Audio as the transport. Derive beat index and cues from audio time. At a fixed 120 BPM in 4/4, measures are two seconds long and a ten-measure block lasts 20 seconds. Start equal-length, tempo-matched original or rights-cleared stems at the same scheduled time. Start the chorus stem muted and ramp gain at the next schedulable measure after unlock. Stamp camera frames in a monotonic browser timebase and map them into audio time before comparing timestamps. Keep inference time out of the audio scheduling path. [W3C Web Audio specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio-1.0/)
3. **Session and feedback.** Track active player, block phase, movement variant, effort choice and accrued participation centrally. Only the active player accrues time during a running block, including in camera-free mode. Pause and handoff stop accrual. Pose feedback can show uncertainty while the running block continues. Keep the meter monotonic and show the reason for a change, for example “You chose Easier. Next up is a smaller press.” Apply the player's mode and movement exclusions before selecting any variant. The same beat drives both variants. See [the PULSE blueprint](BLUEPRINT.md) and [the sync and scoring research](../possible%20implementation/technical_and_scientific_research_sync_points_health.md).
4. **Scope.** Finish the two-turn loop, a clear adaptation, a real audiovisual chorus and a recap first. A demo preset can use two 20-second turns, a 40-second participation threshold and an illustrated recap. Mark the shorter preset as the demo session. Defer remote rooms, leaderboard, broader move library, account sync and ghost replays until after the core rehearsal.

### One 45-minute test on the judging laptop

- **First 15 minutes.** Get a volunteer and a physical broadcast-capable device. Try desktop Chrome on localhost or the intended HTTPS origin. Use the chooser from a button click, request the Heart Rate Service (`0x180D`), connect and subscribe to Heart Rate Measurement (`0x2A37`). Decode 8- or 16-bit BPM based on the characteristic flags and timestamp packet receipt. Keep the data in memory and tie the connection to the consenting wearer. [Chrome Web Bluetooth guide](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/capabilities/bluetooth) · [Bluetooth SIG Heart Rate Service](https://www.bluetooth.com/specifications/specs/heart-rate-service-1-0/)
- **Next 30 minutes.** Verify a real stream while camera and audio run together. Acceptance target: three consecutive connections within 20 seconds each, then 60 seconds of live packets during movement without a gap greater than five seconds. On a forced disconnect or five seconds without packets, clear “Live” and show the actual status. Verify re-pairing and a complete no-device run. If the device belongs to one player, do not display its BPM as the other player's during the handoff.
- **Ship decision.** A pass earns a small optional chip in the rehearsed demo. Otherwise cut it from the judging build. A simulated device can test the interface; label it “Simulated” and keep it out of the live integration claim. Keep BPM out of scoring, adaptation, judging footage and the shared recap unless the wearer has specifically agreed to show it. No vendor login or upload is required for this Bluetooth path.

## Judging run of show

- **30-second video.** Show the standing/seated pair immediately, the effort choice and new move, then the audible Shared Chorus and combined recap.
- **90 seconds live.** 0–10s explain the shared workout and start the preloaded session. 10–30s show the standing block. 30–40s request Easier and show the next-variant reason and player handoff. 40–60s show the seated player's turn and the chorus unlock. 60–75s show the recap and mention the verified wearable only if it is part of the run. 75–90s connect the demo to People First and explain the local processing. During rehearsal, verify the Easier choice produces a distinct visible cue for the same player's next turn; preload a short continuation or show that preview during the handoff.
- **Two-minute demo or three-minute version.** Use the extra 30 seconds to show the original player's adapted turn. For the three-minute presentation, add a camera-loss recovery and a short walk-through of the audio clock and participation meter.
- **Two-minute Q&A.** Prepare concise answers on two people sharing one camera, what the app measures, where personal data goes, how a failed sensor behaves, and what user testing would substantiate the Health track.
